Architecture & Theory

Structural Reasoning

Instead of regressing a final cost, our Pointer Network learns the split policy of dynamic programming. It points to optimal k-values, ensuring mathematically valid cost construction by design.

Curriculum Learning

Models were trained using a staged approach (n=5, 15, 30, 50). This allows the neural weights to stabilize on simpler sub-structures before generalizing to the exponential complexity of long chains.
